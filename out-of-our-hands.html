Out of Our Hands

If the designer writes the rules, who authors the form? Draw or upload a signature, then change the three rules and watch the form leave your hand.

Cohesion: each point steers toward the centre of the points near it.
Separation: points back away from neighbours that get too close.
Mimicry: each point turns toward the average heading of its neighbours.
A pull back toward where each point started. Zero lets the form drift free.
